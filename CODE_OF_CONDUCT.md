Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Intent FreeDomain project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or other personal characteristics.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, and community members to participate respectfully and constructively.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and working toward solutions.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or sensitive information about others without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the Intent FreeDomain maintainers.

For project-related concerns, contact:

Email: admin@int.yt

Please provide enough information for the maintainers to understand and investigate the concern. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces associated with Intent FreeDomain.

Enforcement

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to respond to reports fairly and promptly. Depending on the severity and circumstances, violations may result in a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and is adapted for the Intent FreeDomain project.
